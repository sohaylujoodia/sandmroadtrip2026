Before You Go

Know Before
You Drive

📋
Book Hotels Now

The Ahwahnee, El Tovar and Zion Lodge require months of advance booking. You're 6 weeks out — do this today. Yosemite timed entry permits open on recreation.gov 4 weeks before arrival.

🎫
America the Beautiful Pass

$80 per vehicle, covers all 6 parks on this trip. Individual entrance fees would total ~$175. Buy at usgs.gov or at the first park gate. One pass covers everyone in the car.

🌡
Death Valley Heat

July temperatures regularly exceed 115°F (46°C). Walk only before 9am or after 6pm. Carry serious water. Never leave the car running unattended. This is not an exaggeration.

🚗
Car Rental

Book a mid-size SUV minimum — mountain roads, desert highways and some unpaved tracks. Enrol your Amex Platinum in Hertz Gold Five Star or National Emerald Executive for a free upgrade.

🥾
Permits Required

Yosemite Valley timed entry permit (recreation.gov), Angels Landing in Zion (recreation.gov), and Half Dome if attempting it all require advance permits. Book as soon as windows open.

🎒
Pack for Both

55°F alpine nights in Sequoia to 115°F desert days in Death Valley. Layers, SPF 50+, good walking shoes, and a large refillable water bottle for every person in the car.
